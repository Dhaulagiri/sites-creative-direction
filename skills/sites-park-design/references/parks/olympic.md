# Olympic

**Thesis:** sheltered layers and clear openings; depth comes from overlapping groups and alternating density.

## Palette seeds

| Ingredient | Hex | Possible role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fir | `#153E35` | Text, navigation, or deep surface |
| Fog | `#E7EEEA` | Open surface |
| Tidal blue | `#245C73` | Action candidate |
| Fern | `#67814B` | Small accent |
| Bark | `#453C35` | Secondary dark |

## Translation

- Organize related material in nested bands or an index beside a detailed reading area. Distinguish layers with alignment and surface shifts rather than endless floating cards.
- Pair rounded humanist body text with sturdy headings; let indentation and weight express depth.
- Use close spacing inside collections and clear openings between them.
- Favor broad continuous surfaces, inset boundaries, and modest rounding only where it clarifies containment.
- Signature detail: an inset secondary band that follows a collection through expanded and collapsed states.

**Avoid:** leaf decoration, green gradients everywhere, blurred/glassy panels, or layers that obscure relationships.

**Mobile:** flatten side-by-side layers into clearly labeled sections; keep selection state and context visible.
