# White Sands

**Thesis:** a continuous open surface with fine directional cues; hierarchy emerges through proportion and interruption.

## Palette seeds

| Ingredient | Hex | Possible role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Gypsum | `#FAF9F3` | Main surface |
| Shadow | `#36465A` | Main text |
| Dusk | `#6D607D` | Action candidate |
| Pale blue | `#DBE5EE` | Secondary surface |
| Clay | `#9B553C` | Sparse accent |

## Translation

- Favor a continuous reading plane or a focused workspace with a small secondary rail. Do not divide every paragraph or control group into a box.
- Let a readable editorial face or generously proportioned sans carry the hierarchy; use consistent line lengths and strong text contrast.
- Create wide outer breathing room but keep related controls close. Empty space must not delay the main task.
- Prefer fine rules, shallow inset surfaces, and broad calm boundaries over shadows.
- Signature detail: a subtle curved section boundary in a nonessential margin, if the surface benefits from one.

**Avoid:** invisible controls, low-contrast pale typography, giant empty heroes, or applying a cream/serif recipe to every other park.

**Mobile:** tighten margins, retain the reading measure, and bring secondary tools into the normal content flow.
