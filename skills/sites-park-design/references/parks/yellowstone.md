# Yellowstone

**Thesis:** a calm field punctuated by concentrated intensity; most of the surface stays quiet so a few changes matter.

## Palette seeds

| Ingredient | Hex | Possible role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ink | `#222D34` | Main text or deep surface |
| Mineral | `#E7B936` | Highlight fill with dark text |
| Pool | `#117B83` | Action candidate |
| Pale stone | `#F0F2EB` | Main surface |
| Copper | `#B65B33` | Secondary accent |

## Translation

- Use a broad quiet reading or data plane with one focal region. Highlight actual selected or changed content rather than decorating every item.
- Keep body typography neutral and legible; distinguish focal values through scale and weight.
- Preserve steady row rhythm around a deliberately larger selected or summary area.
- Use clean boundaries with occasional nested rings around an actual focus region, if they aid orientation.
- Signature detail: a concentrated accent band within the selected region, echoed in the selection control.

**Avoid:** rainbow gradients, glowing status dots, yellow body text, fake heat maps, or assigning mineral colors to statuses without checking meaning.

**Mobile:** keep the focal content near the relevant control; reduce secondary accents rather than shrinking the data.
