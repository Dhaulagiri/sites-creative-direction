# Hawaiʻi Volcanoes

**Thesis:** broad dark masses separated by precise seams; a small amount of luminous color has substantial weight.

## Translation

- Create connected slabs of information with a strong reading or working axis. Reserve one luminous seam for the primary action or selected region.
- Use decisive contemporary headings and a practical body sans; monospaced details only where values or technical content warrant them.
- Alternate compact working groups with firm section breaks. Drama comes from contrast and mass, not enormous gaps.
- Favor flat, firm surfaces and thin separators; adjust palette roles if the user requires a light theme.
- Signature detail: a narrow warm seam along the active region, echoed in keyboard focus with sufficient contrast.

**Avoid:** lava animations, flame illustrations, glowing everything, inaccessible orange-on-white text, or decorative use of cultural motifs.

**Mobile:** retain clear reading order and one active accent; do not rely on hover to reveal important controls.
