# Acadia

**Thesis:** compact clusters against a clear horizon; sturdy divisions make a practical surface feel precise.

## Palette seeds

| Ingredient | Hex | Possible role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Atlantic | `#174C67` | Actions or navigation |
| Granite | `#D6DCDD` | Secondary surface |
| Spruce | `#183F38` | Text or deep band |
| White | `#F7FAF9` | Main surface |
| Buoy | `#B74632` | Sparse emphasis |

## Translation

- Anchor the page with a useful horizontal control band; place compact groups below it using shared baselines and clear gutters.
- Use sturdy slab-like headings selectively, with a clear sans body and tabular data. Editorial rhythm can come from rules and aligned labels.
- Keep working areas efficient, separated by larger consistent gutters rather than individual card shadows.
- Use precise rectangular boundaries and subtle curves only where they connect related areas.
- Signature detail: a strong horizon rule with compact section labels aligned to it.

**Avoid:** anchors, ropes, nautical badges, faux park signage, or turning every cluster into a rounded card.

**Mobile:** wrap the control band into a compact stack; maintain predictable alignment and make frequently used controls easy to reach.
