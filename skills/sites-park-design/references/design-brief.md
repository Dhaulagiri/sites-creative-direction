# Saved design brief

Save roughly 200–350 words at `design/park-direction.md`. This records decisions for continuity, not a user questionnaire or public page.

Use these fields in plain Markdown:

- **Park and product fit:** selected profile, primary task, audience, and why its spatial character helps.
- **UX model:** primary task pattern, useful outcome, and the essential information/action/result loop. Note required context and continuity only where relevant; do not invent capabilities.
- **Concept selected:** the concrete arrangement of information; note the two alternatives in one sentence each and why this one won. Alternatives must differ structurally.
- **First viewport:** useful content and controls immediately present on desktop and narrow screens.
- **Palette roles:** surface, text, muted text, action, accent, border, and semantic status colors. Include actual hex values and measured contrast for implemented text pairs when checked. Park accents never replace unambiguous status semantics.
- **Typography:** hierarchy and face characteristics, with available font choices/fallbacks. No tiny metadata or unreadable display fonts on controls.
- **Composition and rhythm:** grouping, alignment, relative spacing, density, and responsive transformation.
- **Signature detail:** one repeatable profile-specific treatment and where it belongs. Optional on a highly constrained utilitarian surface.
- **Avoid:** two likely ways this direction could collapse into a generic template. Explicitly omit decorative eyebrows and redundant explanatory copy.
- **Validation:** update after rendering with views inspected, concrete findings, fixes, and checks not performed.

Do not record hidden chain-of-thought or extended deliberation. Keep only short design alternatives, decisions, and observable evidence. Respect explicit user requirements even when they depart from the chosen profile.
